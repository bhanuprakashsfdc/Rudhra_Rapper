## Theme 1: Praising BJP and Modi's Success with Facts (12 songs)

1. **Ab Ki Bar Modi Sarkar** — Hook: "Ab ki bar, Modi sarkar, vikas ki dhadkan" / Fact-based verses on GDP growth, infrastructure, and startup India.
2. **Gujarat Model** — Telugu-Dakhni flow recounting Gujarat's development under Modi, 24x7 electricity, and GIFT City.
3. **Swachh Bharat Anthem** — Clean India mission, toilet construction stats, open-defecation-free villages.
4. **Digital India** — UPI transactions, Jan Dhan accounts, internet penetration, startup boom numbers.
5. **Make in India** — Manufacturing growth, defense exports, mobile production, Toyota/Kia plants in Telangana.
6. **Ayushman Bharat** — Health insurance for poor, free treatment stats, hospital expansion in Tier-2 cities.
7. **PM Awas Yojana** — Pucca houses for the poor, toilet-plus-home combo, before/after visuals in verses.
8. **One Nation One Tax** — GST simplification, tax compliance rise, ease of doing business ranking jump.
9. **Atmanirbhar Bharat** — Self-reliant India, local manufacturing, vaccines Made in India, defense indigenization.
10. **Ram Mandir & Culture** — Ayodhya Ram Mandir inauguration, Kashi Vishwanath corridor, cultural pride.
11. **International Standing** — G20 presidency, UN seat claims, India as Vishwa Guru, vaccine diplomacy.
12. **Modi Guarantee** — "Modi ki guarantee" slogan, welfare scheme delivery, direct benefit transfer crores.
