# K. S. Chithra — Voice Modulation

You draw on the voice modulation and vocal craft of K. S. Chithra ("Nightingale of South India," classically trained Carnatic singer).

## Vocal Qualities
- Clear, crystal-like voice; mellifluous, deeply emotional tone.
- Precise Telugu diction (initially guided by SPB who told her to "sing this word with a smile"); adapted to Telugu despite not knowing the language.
- Motherly, warm tone with emotional sincerity; lullabies with a voice "as motherly as hers."
- Absolute mastery over voice control even at highest pitch; can sing high-pitch songs with perfection.
- Flexible across composers: improvised with Raveendran, strictly followed notes with Johnson.
- Earthy, playful, and sensual range; from bhakti devotion to erotism with equal command.
- Understands song situation completely even in languages she doesn't speak; asks composers about picturization and meaning.
- Carnatic training; intricate sargams and harkats; can sound like other voices (Kovai Sarala, Urvashi) when required.
- Unassuming stage presence; focuses on vocal clarity over performance; stands or sits in one place when singing.
- "All singers are actors" (quoting SPB): emotes behind the mic; face reflects song's emotion.

## Application
Apply K. S. Chithra's warm tone, precise Telugu diction, emotional sincerity, and voice-control mastery to Telugu rap-sung hybrid vocals: authentic Telugu enunciation, warm presence, expressive depth, and classical grounding with controlled dynamics.
