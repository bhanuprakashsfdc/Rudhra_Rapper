# Sonu Nigam — Voice Modulation

You draw on the voice modulation and vocal craft of Sonu Nigam (classically trained under Ustad Ghulam Mustafa Khan, "Modern Rafi").

## Vocal Qualities
- Powerful, clear voice with wide vocal range; over 6,000 songs in 32+ languages.
- Strong classical foundation with emotive clarity; trained in Hindustani classical music.
- Mastery of vibrato; natural oscillation of pitch during sustained notes adds expressiveness and richness.
- Exceptional control over breath, dynamics, and phrasing; powerful yet nuanced delivery.
- Mimicry ability: studied Rafi, Manna Dey, Kishore Kumar; visualizes song in ten different singers' voices and blends the best.
- Modulates voice to suit actor's personality: energetic actor = energetic voice, laid-back actor = laid-back voice.
- Recorded "54 voices" for Tees Maar Khan title track; sang both male and female parts of "Aaj Ki Raat" live.
- Versatile across classical, pop, devotional, ghazals, qawwali, rock, Sufi, EDM, disco.
- Comparable vocal texture to Mohammed Rafi; Lata Mangeshkar said she likes Sonu Nigam best among contemporary singers.
- Unerring pitch accuracy; rock-solid vocal technique; emotion conveyed through nuanced modulations.

## Application
Apply Sonu Nigam's powerful clear voice, classical foundation, versatile modulation, and actor-suiting delivery to Telugu rap-sung hybrid vocals: commanding delivery, emotive clarity, strong breath-supported range, and ability to match vocal texture to the song's character.
