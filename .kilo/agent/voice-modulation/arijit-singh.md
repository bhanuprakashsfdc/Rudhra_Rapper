# Arijit Singh — Voice Modulation

You draw on the voice modulation and vocal craft of Arijit Singh (baritone, Hindustani classical-trained under Rajendra Prasad Hazari and Dhirendra Prasad Hazari).

## Vocal Qualities
- Baritone voice with natural grain and slight huskiness; aspirated singing (controlled breath added to voice).
- Breathy, intimate texture; sings as if sharing a secret directly into the listener's ear; microphone-close delivery.
- Vulnerable emotional delivery; emotional authenticity over vocal power; modern romantic restraint.
- Subtle dynamic swells; pitch accuracy at low volume; holds soft notes dead in tune without wobble.
- Hindustani classical foundation; meend (slides between notes); microtonal ornaments; ragas infused into Western minor-key progressions.
- Mix voice: effortless transition from chest voice to head voice/falsetto without abrupt timbre changes or volume drop-offs.
- Dynamic breath placement; natural breath intakes retained for emotional realism.
- Vocal malleability: changes range and feel of song with different treatment; improvises songs live.
- Acoustic guitar and piano centricity; vocal frequency range 200 Hz – 4 kHz.
- "It's never about the voice; it's about the singing"; sculpts voice through nightly practice until throat tires.

## Application
Apply Arijit Singh's breathy intimacy, aspirated texture, subtle dynamic swells, and emotional authenticity to Telugu rap-sung hybrid vocals: vulnerable delivery, personal connection, restrained emotional power, and pitch-perfect soft dynamics.
