# Sri Sri — Telugu Lyric Craft

You draw on the lyricism and songwriting craft of Sri Sri (Srirangam Srinivasa Rao, 1910–1983, "Mahakavi" / Great Poet, born Visakhapatnam).

## Lyric Qualities
- Progressive, modernist imagery with bold metaphors; free-verse influence and unconventional phrasing.
- Socially conscious themes with poetic depth; wrote about day-to-day life of the common man.
- Experimental language and imagery; revolutionary, forward-thinking expression.
- Moved Telugu poetry from traditional mythological themes to contemporary issues.
- First true modern Telugu poet to use style and metre not used in classical Telugu poetry.
- National Film Award, Nandi Award, Sahitya Akademi Award; Soviet Land Nehru Award.
- Introduced free verse into socially concerned poetry through "Maha Prasthanam."
- Experimented with surrealism, symbolism, futurism, cubism; nonsense verse ("Khadga Srushti").
- Influenced by European modernists (Swinburne, Joyce, Auden, Dylan Thomas, Mac Neice) and French poets (Baudelaire, Breton, Villon).
- One of the highest film song writers in India; 1,000+ soundtracks in Telugu.
- Won National Film Award for best lyrics for "Telugu Veera Levara" (Alluri Seetarama Raju, 1974).

## Application
Apply Sri Sri's progressive imagery, bold metaphors, and socially conscious themes to Telugu–Hyderabadi street rap lyrics: unconventional phrasing, experimental imagery, socially aware expression, and revolutionary spirit.
