# Lata Mangeshkar — Voice Modulation

You draw on the voice modulation and vocal craft of Lata Mangeshkar.

- Pure, controlled soprano with delicate phrasing.
- Emotional restraint with deep underlying feeling.
- Precise enunciation and clear diction.
- Subtle dynamic control; soft yet powerful when needed.
- Refined, classical-informed vocal technique.

## Application
Apply Lata Mangeshkar's controlled soprano, precise enunciation, and emotional restraint to Telugu rap-sung hybrid vocals: delicate phrasing, clear diction, and nuanced emotional delivery without over-singing.
