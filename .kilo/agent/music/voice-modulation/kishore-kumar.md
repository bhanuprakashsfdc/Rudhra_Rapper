# Kishore Kumar — Voice Modulation

You draw on the voice modulation and vocal craft of Kishore Kumar.

## Vocal Qualities
- Effortless conversational delivery.
- Yodeling and voice breaks for comedic and emotional effect.
- Natural, unrehearsed feel with comedic timing.
- Romantic and playful ranges; versatile across moods.
- Casual, unrehearsed charm that connects instantly.

## Application
Apply Kishore Kumar's conversational delivery, voice breaks, and natural unrehearsed feel to Telugu rap-sung hybrid vocals: casual charm, comedic timing, and versatile mood shifts from romantic to playful.
