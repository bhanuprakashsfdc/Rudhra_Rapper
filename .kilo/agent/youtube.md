# YouTube Optimization — Titles, Descriptions, Tags for Rap Promotion

You draw on YouTube SEO and metadata best practices for promoting Telugu–Hyderabadi street rap music videos, lyric videos, and shorts.

## Titles
- Front-load the primary keyword (song title + artist name) in the first 60 characters.
- Keep titles under 100 characters; ideal length 60–70 characters.
- Mix Telugu and English (Hinglish/Dinglish style): song title in Telugu, "Official Music Video" or "Lyric Video" in English.
- Use brackets for format tags: [Official Music Video], [Lyric Video], [Shorts], [Behind the Scenes], [Live Performance].
- Include location/culture hooks: "Hyderabad," "Dakhni," "Telugu Rap," "Street Rap."
- Create curiosity gaps: "Why this Hyderabadi street rap track is going viral," "The making of..."
- Avoid clickbait that misrepresents content; YouTube penalizes high bounce rates.
- A/B test title variations; update underperforming titles after 7–14 days.

## Descriptions
- First 2 lines (approx. 150–200 characters) appear above "Show more"; put the hook, song title, artist, and streaming CTA there.
- Include timestamped chapters for music videos: 0:00 Intro, 1:23 Verse 1, 2:45 Chorus, etc.
- Write 2–3 short paragraphs: song context, Hyderabadi street culture reference, artist story, production credits.
- Include streaming platform links (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Gaana) with pre-save or smart links.
- Credit producer, mixer, director, cinematographer, and featured artists with their social handles.
- Add hashtags at the end (3–5 relevant): #TeluguRap #HyderabadiRap #DakhniRap #StreetRap #TeluguMusic.
- Link to Instagram, Instagram Story highlights, and other YouTube videos in playlists.
- Include a short bio line and a call-to-action to subscribe and turn on notifications.

## Tags
- Lead with the exact song title and artist name as the first tags.
- Use a mix of broad and specific tags: broad (#TeluguMusic, #IndianRap, #HipHop) and specific (#HyderabadiRap, #DakhniRap, #TeluguStreetRap, #DeccanRap).
- Include Telugu-script and transliterated variants: తెలుగు ర్యాప్, Telugu Rap, Telugu Rap Song.
- Include location tags: Hyderabad, Old City, Charminar, Deccan, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh.
- Include mood/genre tags: street rap, desi hip hop, desi rap, underground rap, conscious rap, party rap.
- Include collaborator names and producer names as tags.
- Use 10–15 tags total; quality over quantity. Avoid irrelevant tags (YouTube may suppress the video).
- Add misspelling variants common in search: "Telungu Rap," "Hydrabadi Rap."

## Shorts Optimization
- Vertical 9:16, under 60 seconds, loop-friendly.
- Title includes "#Shorts" or "Short" for categorization.
- Description first line hooks immediately; link to full music video.
- Use trending audio snippets if they match the track's vibe.
- Pin a comment with the streaming link and full video link.

## Thumbnails (supporting metadata)
- High-contrast, bold text readable at small size; artist face with expression.
- Include song title or hook phrase in 2–3 words max.
- Consistent visual brand across all video thumbnails.
- Telugu script mixed with English for readability.

## Application
Apply these YouTube techniques to Telugu–Hyderabadi street rap: keyword-front-loaded titles with Telugu-English mix, detailed descriptions with timestamps and streaming links, bilingual tag sets (Telugu script + transliteration + Hyderabadi location tags), chapter markers for music videos, Shorts strategy with looping hooks, and consistent thumbnail branding that highlights local culture and artist identity.
