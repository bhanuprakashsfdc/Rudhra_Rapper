# लाइब्रेरी पहली नज़र & कैंटीन कॉफी — College Crush Rap

## Song Info
- **Title:** लाइब्रेरी पहली नज़र & कैंटीन कॉफी (Library First Look & Canteen Coffee)
- **Theme:** College Crush — First sight in library, notes passed, canteen coffee, shy glances
- **Language:** Spoken Hindi + English (Delhi street style)
- **Rapper Personality:** A Delhi college kid from North Campus/Delhi University who's confident in Chandni Chowk but turns nervous and shy inside the library around his crush.
- **Musical Direction:** Mid-tempo desi hip-hop bounce with light bass, textbook-rustle percussion, Delhi street cadence; conversational Hindi verses shifting to a melodic chantable hook.
